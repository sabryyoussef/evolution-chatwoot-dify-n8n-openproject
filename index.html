Where humans and automation see the same conversation. Account 2, inbox 2. Labels such as dev-needed, cyclex, ai-create-wp drive n8n.

n8n — the orchestrator

n8n is the only layer allowed to execute. It receives Chatwoot webhooks, calls Dify, then creates OpenProject work packages and optionally replies on WhatsApp.

Open n8n chatwoot-ai-analysis
Do not fire production webhooks in a demo. Opening the editor is safe. Triggering the workflow creates real work packages.

Dify — AI understanding

Dify classifies the message, picks the project, extracts OpenProject fields, and drafts a short Arabic reply. It does not create tasks. n8n consumes its JSON.

Open Dify Studio Platform Copilot

OpenProject — source of truth

Every accepted bug, feature, and follow-up lives here. Unmapped WhatsApp work lands in WhatsApp Queue (project 14).
